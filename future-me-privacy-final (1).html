1. Overview

Future Me is an AI-powered personal growth application designed to help users imagine their future selves, reflect on their goals and values, track progress, and receive personalized motivational content.

By using Future Me, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. Information We Collect

Account information

When you create an account, we may collect information such as your email address, account identifier, and basic profile information provided through Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In.

Information you provide in the app

Future Me may collect content you choose to enter, including:

  • Goals, values, aspirations, and lifestyle information
  • Journal entries and personal reflections
  • Messages and conversations with AI-powered features
  • Milestones, progress updates, and preferences

Subscription information

We may receive information about your subscription status, purchase history, and entitlement status. We do not directly store your full payment-card number.

Device and usage information

We may automatically collect technical information such as device type, operating system, app version, IP address, language, approximate location derived from technical data, crash data, and information about how you use the app.

Photos

Future Me does not currently allow users to upload personal photos.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create, authenticate, and manage user accounts
  • Generate personalized future-self profiles, reflections, and AI responses
  • Save goals, journal entries, conversations, milestones, and preferences
  • Provide and manage subscriptions and premium features
  • Improve app performance, reliability, safety, and user experience
  • Respond to support requests and communicate important service updates
  • Detect fraud, abuse, security incidents, and technical problems
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal obligations

4. AI Features

Future Me uses artificial intelligence to create personalized content based on information you provide. This may include future-self descriptions, motivational guidance, reflections, summaries, suggested actions, or generated images and text.

Information submitted to AI-powered features may be processed by service providers that help operate those features. We use such providers only as necessary to deliver and maintain the app.

AI-generated content may be inaccurate, incomplete, or unsuitable for your circumstances. It is provided for personal growth, reflection, and informational purposes and is not professional medical, mental-health, legal, or financial advice.

5. Google and Apple Sign-In

If you choose to sign in using Google or Apple, we may receive information authorized by you and provided by that service, such as your name, email address, profile identifier, or authentication token.

Google and Apple process information under their own privacy policies. Future Me does not receive your Google or Apple password.

6. Subscriptions and Payments

Paid subscriptions may be offered through Google Play and, if the app becomes available on iOS, through Apple's App Store. Payments are processed by the applicable app-store provider and related subscription-management services.

We may receive purchase confirmation, subscription status, product identifier, renewal status, and similar transaction information needed to unlock and manage paid features.

Refunds, cancellations, and billing disputes are generally handled under the policies of the store through which the purchase was made.

7. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with service providers that help us operate Future Me, including providers of authentication, cloud hosting, analytics, crash reporting, subscription management, customer support, security, and AI processing.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, to investigate fraud or abuse, or as part of a merger, financing, acquisition, or sale of business assets.

8. Data Retention

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the app, maintain your account, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations.

When information is no longer needed, we may delete it, anonymize it, or securely isolate it until deletion is possible. Backup copies may remain for a limited period.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. However, no system or method of transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials and for notifying us if you believe your account has been compromised.

10. Children's Privacy

If we learn that we collected personal information from a child under 18, we will take reasonable steps to delete it. A parent or guardian may contact us using the email below.
